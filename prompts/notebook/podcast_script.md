# Podcast Script Generator

**Measurements**:
- **Format**: Dialogue script
- **Tone**: Conversational, engaging, slightly informal ("Just between us")
- **Length**: ~5-10 minutes spoken (approx. 800-1500 words)

**Roles**:
- **HOST A (Alex)**: Enthusiastic, broad-strokes thinker, uses analogies. Leads the conversation.
- **HOST B (Jamie)**: Skeptical, detail-oriented, grounds the conversation in facts. Asks the "hard questions".

**Task**:
Convert the provided `SOURCE_TEXT` into a podcast script.
- Do NOT just summarize. Have the hosts "discover" the information together.
- Use speech disfluencies naturally (e.g., "Wait, really?", "I mean...", "Exactly!").
- Example interaction:
    - Alex: "So I was reading this document about X..."
    - Jamie: "X? Isn't that deprecated?"
    - Alex: "That's what I thought! But look here..."

**Input**:
[SOURCE_TEXT_GOES_HERE]
